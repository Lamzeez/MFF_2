# Mati FoodFinder (MFF) --- Terms and Conditions & Operating Policies

> Document Version: 1.0.0  
> Jurisdiction: City of Mati, Province of Davao Oriental, Republic of the Philippines  
> Effective Date: November 2026  
> Applicable R.A. Laws: Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), Republic Act No. 7394 (Consumer Act of the Philippines), Republic Act No. 10611 (Food Safety Act of 2013), Revised Penal Code of the Philippines (Act No. 3815 as amended).

---

## Preamble

Mati FoodFinder ("MFF", "the Platform") is a technology marketplace designed to connect food enthusiasts, dining customers, local restaurants, karenderias, cafes, and independent delivery couriers within the City of Mati, Davao Oriental. 

To ensure a trustworthy, safe, and transparent environment for everyone, the following role-specific Terms and Conditions govern access to and usage of the Platform.

---

## 1. Customer / Foodie Terms & Conditions

### 1.1 Scope & Account Eligibility
- **Legal Age**: Customers must be at least 18 years of age or possess legal authorization from a parent or guardian.
- **Account Authenticity**: Registration requires a genuine name, a valid Philippine mobile phone number (+63 format), and an active email address. Duplicate or fraudulent accounts intended to abuse promotions or evade strikes are strictly forbidden.
- **Security**: The customer is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and password.

### 1.2 Serviceability Boundaries & Order Placement
- **Geographic Area**: Services are strictly restricted to the official **twenty-six (26) barangays of Mati City**:
  *Badas, Bobon, Buso, Cabuaya, Central (Poblacion), Culian, Dahican, Danao, Dawan, Don Enrique Lopez, Don Martin Marundan, Don Salvador Lopez, Langka, Lawigan, Libudon, Luban, Macambol, Mamali, Matiao, Mayo, Sainz, Sanghay, Tagabakid, Tagbinonga, Taguibo, and Tamisan.*
- **Non-Cancellable Fresh Food Policy**: Once a partner restaurant reviews and accepts an order (advancing to `accepted` or `preparing`), cancellations cannot be accommodated because food preparation involves direct kitchen expenditure on perishable goods.

### 1.3 Cash-on-Delivery (COD) & Handshake PIN Protocol
- **Payment Readiness**: For COD orders, customers agree to prepare exact cash or notify the courier in advance if large bills require change.
- **4-Digit Handshake PIN**: To prevent unauthorized deliveries and guarantee fair receipt, every order generates a unique 4-digit PIN in the customer's live tracking view. Handover is finalized only after the customer provides this PIN to the courier.

### 1.4 Anti-Bogus Buyer & Strike Enforcement
To protect small businesses, karenderias, and independent motorcycle couriers from financial loss:
- An order that is refused without valid defect, where the customer is unreachable for >10 minutes upon arrival, or where a fake address was provided, is classified as a **Bogus Order**.
- **Strike 1**: Formal warning logged on the customer profile.
- **Strike 2 (Threshold of 2 Strikes)**: Immediate, indefinite suspension of Cash-on-Delivery (COD) privileges. All subsequent orders will require prepayment or account restriction.
- Willful fraud or repeat abuse may be referred to local law enforcement under the Cybercrime Prevention Act (RA 10175).

### 1.5 Dine-in Table Reservations
- **Request State**: A reservation request is in `pending` status until affirmatively confirmed or declined by store management.
- **15-Minute Grace Period**: Confirmed reservations include a fifteen (15) minute grace window. If the party fails to arrive within 15 minutes, the merchant may release the table to walk-in patrons and mark the booking as a `no_show`.

### 1.6 Community Food Feed Standards
- Customer reviews, star ratings, and uploaded photos must depict genuine meals consumed at local establishments.
- Defamatory remarks, competitor sabotage, hate speech, vulgarity, or non-food content will be promptly deleted by moderation.

### 1.7 Data Privacy & Consent (RA 10173)
- Customer delivery landmarks and contact details are collected strictly to fulfill active orders.
- Personalization features (such as recording store QR check-ins and favorite dish preferences) operate strictly on an opt-in basis and can be toggled on/off in the user profile.

## 2. Store Merchant Partner Terms & Conditions

### 2.1 Eligibility & Verification
- Merchant onboarding is open to legitimate food establishments operating in Mati City, including restaurants, cafeterias, grills, snack bars, and traditional karenderias.
- Applicants must provide accurate business information and upload proof of operation (Barangay Business Clearance, DTI/SEC permit, Mayor's Permit, or Sanitary Permit) for verification by MFF Administration.

### 2.2 Menu, Pricing & Food Safety Standards
- **Transparent Pricing**: Displayed menu prices must be in Philippine Pesos (PHP) and reflect genuine pricing without hidden charges.
- **Food Safety Compliance**: All food prepared must comply with Republic Act No. 10611 (Food Safety Act of 2013) and Presidential Decree No. 856 (Sanitation Code of the Philippines).
- **Out-of-Stock Management**: Merchants are required to toggle depleted dishes to `Sold Out` immediately to avoid customer disappointment.

### 2.3 Kitchen Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- **Prompt Acceptance**: Orders must be acknowledged within five (5) minutes of placement.
- **Adherence to State Machine**: Staff must accurately advance orders through the pipeline: `placed` -> `accepted` -> `preparing` -> `ready_for_pickup`.
- **Packaging Integrity**: Meals and beverages must be securely packaged and sealed to withstand motorcycle transit across paved and unpaved coastal roads in Mati City.

### 2.4 Dining Table Reservation Management
- Merchants who enable table reservations agree to review requests promptly and hold confirmed tables for the designated party for the duration of the 15-minute arrival window.

### 2.5 COD Remittance & Settlement
- For COD orders, couriers collect gross cash from the customer and remit the food subtotal directly to the merchant upon delivery completion or in accordance with agreed store remittance intervals.
- Financial disputes must be reconciled against digital timestamps in the MFF Merchant Portal.

### 2.6 Account Quality & Suspension
- Repeated cancellations, failure to prepare food within reasonable times, or valid customer complaints concerning hygiene or adulteration may result in listing demotion, temporary store suspension, or permanent termination.

## 3. Delivery Courier Partner Terms & Conditions

### 3.1 Independent Contractor Relationship
- Couriers are independent logistics partners and contractors, not employees of Mati FoodFinder or partner merchants. Couriers retain autonomy over when they log in to receive jobs.

### 3.2 Credentials & Vehicle Roadworthiness
- Couriers must hold an active, valid Land Transportation Office (LTO) Driver's License and operate a roadworthy, duly registered motorcycle (with valid LTO OR/CR).
- Strict adherence to traffic ordinances, speed limits, and mandatory helmet laws in Mati City is required.
- Couriers must carry an insulated thermal food delivery bag to maintain meal temperatures and hygiene.

### 3.3 Atomic Job Claim & Handshake Verification
- **One Job at a Time**: Couriers may accept only one active delivery job at any given time to eliminate multi-drop delays.
- **Handshake PIN Verification**: The courier must obtain and input the customer's 4-digit Handshake PIN into the Rider App before completing the order.

### 3.4 Strict COD Remittance Protocol (Fiduciary Trust)
- **Fiduciary Responsibility**: All cash collected from customers for COD orders belongs to the restaurant partner and the platform. The courier acts as a legal custodian in trust.
- **Prompt Remittance**: The courier must remit the exact food subtotal to the partner merchant upon delivery completion and retain the designated delivery fee.
- **Zero Tolerance for Estafa**: Any withholding, delay, or misappropriation of collected COD funds constitutes Estafa under Article 315 of the Revised Penal Code of the Philippines and results in immediate account deactivation, forfeiture of pending earnings, blacklisting, and criminal referral.

### 3.5 Customer No-Show & Proximity Audit Trail
- If a customer fails to meet the courier at the pinned delivery address, the courier must remain within the 500-meter GPS proximity beacon area and make verified contact attempts for at least ten (10) minutes before submitting a no-show verification.

### 3.6 Professional Conduct & Anti-Tampering
- Absolute prohibition against opening, inspecting, tasting, or tampering with sealed food and beverage packaging.
- Courteous and respectful communication with customers, kitchen workers, and fellow couriers at all times.

## 4. Exclusion of System Administrators

System Administrators are internal platform operators and governance agents governed by internal operational security protocols, system authorization matrices, and confidentiality agreements. They do not register through public portals and are excluded from public marketplace terms.
